## Cross-site observations (evidence)

Hero patterns: Outcome-led heroes dominate among converters (Parloa loyalty, fonio "never misses a call", Sidora "Zeit spart", Cognigy "Say Goodbye to Poor Customer Service", Glean "understands your company"). Technology-led heroes appear at Synthflow (still paired with "ROI delivered in weeks") and n8n (control as outcome). Values-led hero at Aleph Alpha (sovereignty) works for public sector, not SME. Weakest: Sierra's "Better outcomes. Built on Sierra." (no concrete promise) and CODING9 (no hero).

CTA pairs: The most common winning pair is a low-commitment primary plus a human secondary: "Start free trial" + "Talk to sales" (Langdock), "Kostenlos testen" + "Termin vereinbaren" (weclapp, amber), "Create your own AI now" + "Book a demo" + "Test AI call now" (fonio). Agencies use "Kostenlose Erstberatung / Erstgespräch" universally (Sidora, a4ai, apexAI, KI-Agentur, Prozessmeister, CODING9 audit) plus a proof secondary ("Echte Beispiele ansehen", "Unsere Cases ansehen"). Enterprise products use "Book/Get a demo" alone (Parloa, Glean, Decagon, Cognigy, Hebbia), which is fine for enterprise but too high-friction for SME.

Proof density ranking (home page): amber, fonio, Langdock, bexio, Cognigy, Decagon > Glean, Sierra, n8n, Synthflow > apexAI (testimonials only), Sidora (numbers only) > Morningside > KI-Agentur (anonymous) > a4ai, CODING9 (none).

Numbers that recur and are therefore expected: time saved per user/week (Glean 1.5 to 3h, a4ai 5 to 20h), % workload/cost reduction (Parloa 90%, Decagon 65 to 95%, Sidora -92%), time to go-live (TIME 3 weeks, Sidora 2 to 4 weeks, fonio "20 minutes", apexAI "Resultate ab Tag 30"), customer counts (bexio 100'000, sevdesk 170.000, KLARA 50'000, amber 400+, fonio 10,000+).

DACH trust signals actually shown: hosting sentence with location (weclapp Frankfurt RZ, bexio/KLARA "Schweizer Rechenzentren", Langdock "servers inside the EU" via Azure, amber Germany, Sidora "CH-Hosting wahlweise" Zürich/Genf, apexAI "keine Daten auf US-Servern ohne explizite Zustimmung"); ISO 27001 (bexio, Langdock, amber, weclapp, Parloa, Synthflow, Glean, Sierra); DSGVO/revDSG/nDSG wording (Sidora, KI-Agentur, CODING9, amber, apexAI); EU AI Act badge (fonio, Sierra, Sidora); local regulatory keywords (Swissdec, QR-Rechnung, GoBD, E-Rechnung EN 16931, DATEV); "Swiss Made Software" badge (bexio, a4ai); local bank/partner logos (bexio 65 banks, KLARA 40+); AV-Vertrag/DPA mention (CODING9, Synthflow MSA/DPA); "Made in Germany" (KI-Agentur, CODING9, Aleph Alpha).

Products vs services separation: apexAI (four named lines with prefixes), CODING9 (Leistungen vs Produkte & Lösungen), Langdock/amber (Platform grid: Chat, Workflows, Agents, Search). Agencies without products do not need it; utxo does.

Demos: live phone number to call the agent (fonio) is unique and fits Call Assistant. Short explainer videos (bexio 1 min, Langdock 4 min, fonio 100 s, amber 3 clips). Interactive product tours (sevdesk, weclapp). Enterprise products show static thumbnails (Parloa, Cognigy) or nothing (Glean, Decagon). Agencies show no demos at all.

Pricing transparency: full tiers on page (bexio, fonio, Prozessmeister, sevdesk from-prices, KLARA from-prices), price anchors in copy (Sidora "ab CHF 4'500", KI-Agentur "ab ca. 5.000€", Synthflow "start at $30,000 annually"), pricing page only (Langdock, amber, n8n, weclapp, Hebbia), none (Parloa, Cognigy, Glean, Decagon, Aleph Alpha, Morningside, apexAI, a4ai, CODING9). Sierra: pricing model stated (outcome-based) without figures.

Language and tone: Agencies targeting owners use Sie without exception (Sidora, a4ai, apexAI, KI-Agentur, CODING9); Prozessmeister mixes Du (home) and Sie (FAQ). SaaS aimed at very small businesses uses Du (sevdesk, KLARA, weclapp, amber). bexio mixes Sie/Du. Enterprise German products publish in English first (Parloa, Langdock, Synthflow, n8n, Cognigy). Jargon: winners keep it low and explain (KI-Agentur FAQ "Brauche ich technisches Vorwissen? Nein."), losers list frameworks (CODING9: Symfony, Vue, LangChain). Anti-hype honesty lines convert attention: "Wir bauen keine KI für die Schublade" (Sidora), "KI, aber richtig." (apexAI), "We don't just talk AI. We deliver it." (Morningside), "KI ausprobiert – und wieder verworfen?" (amber).

Anti-patterns observed: unfilled counters showing "0+" (KI-Agentur); empty "Was unsere Kunden sagen" block (a4ai); typo in hero (a4ai "druch"); tool-logo walls in place of client logos (a4ai); anonymous case studies (Sidora, KI-Agentur); metric inconsistency between pages (Parloa 179% vs 169%); hidden setup fees revealed mid-scroll (Prozessmeister); competing CTAs with no hierarchy (amber trial vs demo; CODING9 email/phone/form); sub-pages thinner than the homepage (apexAI solutions page has no CTA, no proof); no hero at all (CODING9); event banners pushing the hero down (Parloa, Sierra, Decagon, n8n, Cognigy); high-friction single CTA "Get a demo" without any self-serve or proof path (Glean, Decagon); values-only hero without a promise (Aleph Alpha, Sierra).
