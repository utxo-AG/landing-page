A practical path

Four steps to integrate
AI that lasts.

Integration is less about the model and more about the fit. This is the sequence we use with every client.

01

Assess

Identify one process that is repetitive, rule-heavy and costs real time. Start where the return is clear.

02

Pilot

Build a working version on that single workflow and prove it against real cases, not slides.

03

Integrate

Connect it to the systems the work already lives in: CRM, ERP, inbox and files.

04

Scale & measure

Expand to the next process against a success metric agreed before you start.

Where it fits

Where AI earns its place in daily operations.

The best candidates are repeatable, describable and system-bound. A few common examples.

Inbox & correspondence

Triage, draft and route replies with full context.

Quotes & offers

Assemble accurate offers from your catalogue and rules.

CRM & data entry

Keep records current without manual copy and paste.

Document processing

Extract, check and file information from PDFs and forms.

Research & summaries

Turn scattered sources into a short, sourced brief.

Scheduling & follow-ups

Book, confirm and chase without dropping threads.

Before you start

What separates a useful rollout from a costly one.

01

Data sovereignty

Know where your data lives and whether it trains someone else's model. In the EU this is not optional.

02

Change management

A tool no one adopts delivers nothing. Fit the workflow to the team, not the team to the tool.

03

Vendor lock-in

Favour approaches that stay portable across models and systems, so you keep the freedom to change.

04

A defined success metric

Agree what working means, and how you will measure it, before the build begins.
