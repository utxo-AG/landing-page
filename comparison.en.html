An honest assessment

When is each approach right?

Not every company needs a CoWorker. Not every problem justifies a custom development effort. Here is our honest take.

Off-the-shelf fits when
  • Mainly Microsoft 365, faster writing
  • No specific process to automate
  • Docs, decks and notes: no deep systems
  • Data sovereignty isn't critical
A standard licence is enough.
CoWorker fits when
  • A repeatable process eats time daily
  • Your data lives in CRM, ERP, databases
  • GDPR and sovereignty are non-negotiable
  • You want results, not chat transcripts
