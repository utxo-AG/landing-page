An honest assessment

When is each approach right?

Not every company needs a CoWorker. Not every problem justifies a custom development effort. Here is our honest take.

Off-the-shelf is a good fit when …
  • You primarily use Microsoft 365 and want to speed up writing tasks
  • There is no specific, repeatable process you want to automate
  • Your team primarily produces documents, presentations, and meeting notes — without deep system involvement
  • Data privacy and sovereignty are not critical requirements
CoWorker is a good fit when …
  • A clearly describable process eats time every day (inbox, scheduling, research, quotes, documentation …)
  • Your data lives in your own systems (CRM, ERP, database) and needs to be included
  • GDPR and data sovereignty are non-negotiable
  • You want measurable results — not a chat transcript, but completed work
