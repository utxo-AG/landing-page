Where AI can help

These six examples show the range, not the limits. Every CoWorker is built completely custom, developed for a specific process in your business. How it works →

01
Reception & Inbox

The Reception CoWorker

Takes calls, voicemails, and emails, understands the request, cross-checks it with CRM and calendar, routes it to the right place, books appointments, and escalates urgent matters. Recognizes existing customers along with their open cases.

Ergebnis

Answered inquiries, routed cases with context, booked appointments, escalations when urgent.

02
Sales

The Sales CoWorker

Takes in a request and actively asks the customer for any missing parameters until the basis for calculation is complete. Then it creates the quote, submits it for approval, sends it after confirmation, and follows up.

Ergebnis

Finished, approved quotes. Sent and followed up.

03
New Business

The Lead Research CoWorker

Researches and qualifies potential customers by your criteria: with company data, contact people, and relevant signals. On the order of minutes to hours instead of weeks.

Ergebnis

A structured, prioritized list of qualified contacts.

04
Regulatory

The Compliance CoWorker

Monitors regulatory changes worldwide relevant to your products and markets. When it detects a relevant change, it prepares it as a briefing, classified by risk or opportunity, before it becomes a problem.

Ergebnis

Proactive, classified alerts instead of late surprises.

05
Engineering & Technical

The Technical Review CoWorker

Checks technical drawings against standards, dimensions, and internal consistency, and flags deviations. A reproducible review step that applies the same rigor to every drawing.

Ergebnis

Reviewed drawings with flagged deviations.

06
Knowledge Management

The Document Management CoWorker

Files documents in a structured way, aggregates content across sources, and delivers the relevant information on request, including its source, so every statement stays traceable.

Ergebnis

Order, accessibility, and answers with sources cited.

Security is part of the architecture from day one.

A CoWorker acts within your company. That's why it's built to do only what it's supposed to — and nothing else. The foundation is an orchestrator model: every subtask runs in an isolated session with tightly scoped permissions.

Network isolation

No open internet access. Only explicitly approved services are reachable.

Scoped permissions

Least-privilege per user, trigger, task, and file. No blanket access a manipulated case could exploit.

Protection against manipulation

External content is treated as data, never as a command. An instruction hidden in an email is content to be assessed, not a command to execute.

Controlled tools

Only the tools assigned to the task; every action is fully logged.

  • Ready for the EU AI Act — Meets the transparency and risk-control requirements of the EU AI Act.

  • GDPR — Data is processed and hosted entirely within the EU under GDPR.

  • ISMS — Information security management system, aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

  • Data sovereignty — Private or fully local hosting on request, down to local open-source models.
