Frequently asked questions.

Does the CoWorker replace our legal counsel?

No. The CoWorker watches, triages and recommends next steps. The legal assessment stays with your compliance or legal team. Where it matters, the CoWorker itself recommends checking with counsel.

Which markets and areas of law does the CoWorker cover?

The catalogue holds 89 verified sources for the EU, Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, Austria and the Netherlands. The areas of law follow from your industry, such as the AI Act, the DSA, data protection, advertising law, medical devices, chemicals, packaging or financial supervision. For other markets it searches official domains. You can add your own sources at any time.

Does the CoWorker catch every change?

The CoWorker only reads sources that can be fetched reliably. Some portals block automated access, such as ECHA or Légifrance, and it does not monitor those. If a feed only delivers a teaser, it says so in the report and points to the source.

How do we know a deadline is right?

A deadline only appears if it is stated verbatim in the title, the snippet or the linked document of the source. The CoWorker discards any other date before the report. Every finding links to its source.

How do we receive the reports?

We set that up for your environment during customisation, for example as an email report. You choose rhythm and time zone; the default proposal is Monday at 08:00.

What does the Compliance CoWorker cost?

That depends on markets, sources and delivery into your environment, so we do not publish flat prices. We clarify the scope in the intro call, then you receive an offer.
