Frequently asked questions.

Does the CoWorker send quotes on its own?

No, not without approval. It prepares quotes, briefings and follow-up emails as drafts. Whatever goes to customers is approved by a person on your team.

What happens if an inquiry is missing information?

It asks instead of guessing. Missing details such as material, quantity or delivery date are requested from the customer or your team before it calculates.

Which systems does it work with?

With the systems your sales team uses, such as ERP, CRM, price lists, contracts and email. Which systems are connected, and with which permissions, is agreed with you per use case. Missing interfaces are developed to fit.

How does it know our conditions and special cases?

From your systems and from the rules we define together: price rules, discount limits, framework agreements and exceptions. Anything outside these rules is flagged for approval.

Does it replace our sales team?

No. It takes over lookups, calculation and preparation. Conversations, negotiation and decisions stay with your team.

What does the CoWorker cost?

It depends on the scope, so we don’t quote flat rates. Development for your company is a one-off fixed price that you know before the start. Operation is covered by a subscription.

How quickly is it ready?

Typically as a pilot in about 30 days: workshop, fixed-price offer, development on your real data, a test run alongside your existing process, and go-live. We give you the exact schedule in the offer.

Where is the data processed?

We set the storage location per project to your requirements: Germany, the EU or on-premise. Your data never trains third-party models, as set out in the data processing agreement, which we draft per project for your stack and requirements.
