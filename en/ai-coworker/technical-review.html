Frequently asked questions.

Which documents does the CoWorker review?

Technical drawings, parts lists, specifications, test reports and technical documentation. We clarify with you before the start which document types and systems are involved.

Which standards does it check against?

Against your own rules if you provide them: in-house standards, internal guidelines and checklists. Without your own rules it checks against the standards and rules that apply to technical drawings. The review report does not replace a certification or a confirmation of conformity.

Does it replace the review by our engineers?

No. It takes over the recurring review work and prepares every finding with its location and rule. Whether a finding applies and how it is resolved is decided by your engineers.

How traceable are the findings?

Every finding names the exact location and the rule it was checked against, so it can be verified directly in the document. Because the same rules are applied the same way every time, the review is reproducible.

Does it work with our systems?

Yes. The CoWorker works with the systems where your documents are kept. We set up the connection as part of the development.

What does the CoWorker cost?

It depends on the scope, so we don’t quote flat rates. Developing it for your company is a one-off fixed price that you know before the start. Operation is covered by a subscription.

Where is the data processed?

We set the storage location per project to your requirements: Germany, the EU or on-premise. Your data never trains third-party models, as set out in the data processing agreement, which we draft per project for your stack and requirements.
