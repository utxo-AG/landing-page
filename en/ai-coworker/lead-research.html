Frequently asked questions.

Where does the data come from?

From publicly accessible sources such as the commercial register, company websites, press, job portals, tenders and review platforms. We agree with you which sources are used. Every data point names its source.

Can lead research be used in a GDPR-compliant way?

Yes, if the setup is right. Legal basis, information duties, data minimisation and retention periods are agreed with you before the start. This does not replace a legal assessment of your specific use, and it is not legal advice.

What does the CoWorker cost?

It depends on the scope of the customisation, so we don’t quote flat rates. Customising it for your company is a one-off fixed price that you know before the start. Operation is covered by a subscription.

How quickly is it ready?

The CoWorker is fully developed. What we adapt is the target profile, sources, output format and handover. We give you the schedule in the offer.

Does it replace our sales team?

No. It takes over research and preparation. Outreach, conversations and decisions stay with your team.

Does it work with our CRM?

We set up the handover to your CRM as part of the customisation. Alternatively, your team receives the results in the agreed format.

Where is the data processed?

We set the storage location per project to your requirements: Germany, the EU or on-premise. Your data never trains third-party models, as set out in the data processing agreement, which we draft per project for your stack and requirements.
