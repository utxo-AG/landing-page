Network: approved services only

  • Network isolation

    No open internet access. Only explicitly approved services are reachable.

  • Least privilege

    Permissions per user, trigger, task and file, with no blanket access.

  • Protection against manipulation

    External content is data, never a command. A hidden instruction is assessed, not executed.

  • Complete audit trail

    Every action is logged, and people approve what matters.

Frequently asked questions.

What does an AI CoWorker cost?

It depends on the process, so we don’t quote flat rates. Development is a one-off fixed price that you know before the project starts. Operation is covered by a subscription sized to your usage. Both are set out in the offer you receive within 48 hours of the workshop.

Where is our data processed?

We set the storage location per project to your requirements: Germany, the EU or on-premise. We agree the provider and region for model processing with you as part of the project. Your data never trains third-party models, as set out in the data processing agreement, which we draft per project for your stack and requirements.

Do we need to change our systems?

No. The CoWorker works in the software your team already uses. Each interface is approved per use case. If one is missing, we develop it.

How quickly is a CoWorker ready?

Typically as a pilot after about 30 days. Until then it runs alongside your current process, so your team can review its work before it takes over.

What does the CoWorker do when it doesn’t know something?

It asks instead of guessing. If a detail is missing, it gets it from the customer or your team. If a case is unclear, it hands it to the person in charge with all the information.

Do we need Sokosumi or Masumi for this?

No. Standalone operation is the default, and the CoWorker works fully without Sokosumi and Masumi. On request, we list it on Sokosumi or connect it to other AI agents through Masumi.
