Frequently asked questions.

Does it sound like a person?

It speaks in a natural voice and replies in real time, with no phone menu and no hold music. We agree on voice, tone and greeting with you. The best way to judge is to listen for yourself in the live demo above.

Do callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. At the start of the call the assistant tells callers they are speaking with an AI. This meets the transparency obligation of the EU AI Act. We agree the exact wording with you.

Does it recognise existing customers?

Yes. Through your connected CRM it recognises existing customers and links the call to the open case. We develop the integration individually for your system.

What happens with urgent calls?

You decide what counts as urgent. When the assistant recognises such a case, it forwards the call to your team or alerts the person in charge immediately. Everything else arrives as a ticket with a summary.

Which systems can it connect to?

Your calendar, your CRM and your ticket system, in other words the software your team already uses. If an interface is missing, we develop it.

How is it different from anruf.guru?

anruf.guru is our own product: an answering machine that takes messages and turns them into tickets, appointments and text messages. It does not hold conversations. The AI Phone Assistant talks to your callers, asks follow-up questions and is developed for your business and connected to your systems.
