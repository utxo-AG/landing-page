    Frequently asked questions.

    Question not covered? business@utxo.ag

    Who is this for?

    Mid-sized companies and enterprises in any industry where recurring work ties up time: inquiries, quotes, research, documents, incidents. If AI does not pay off for your process, we will tell you so in the workshop.

    What does an AI CoWorker cost?

    Development is a one-off fixed price, agreed before the project starts. Ongoing operation is covered by a subscription based on your usage profile. Both are tailored to you, and you receive the offer within 48 hours of the workshop.

    Do we need to change our systems?

    No. A CoWorker works in the software your team already uses, such as your ERP, CRM, email and documents. If an interface is missing, we develop it.

    Who runs the solution after go-live?

    We do. Operation, hosting, maintenance and further development are covered by the subscription, so the solution grows with your process.
