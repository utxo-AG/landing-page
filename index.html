Projects

Projects

Masumi & Sokosumi

Serviceplan recognized the shift toward agentic AI early. For the Serviceplan Group we develop Masumi, an on-chain payment and identity network for AI agents, and Sokosumi, a marketing platform where "agentic employees" handle campaign tasks end-to-end under human oversight. We operate both together with Serviceplan (House of Communication) in a GDPR- and EU-AI-Act-compliant setup, and they have since been rolled out to Serviceplan's clients too.

NMKR-Studio

Our first project. We provide infrastructure for the chaotic world of NFTs. A SaaS platform that lets anyone easily create blockchain tokens and sell them conveniently via API or through our own payment portal.

We manage our customers' assets and process millions of requests, some of which reach us without warning. Reliability and resilience have defined our products since day one.

RST Datentechnik

For the regional internet provider RST Datentechnik on the Upper Rhine, we put a CoWorker into operation for outage management. It proactively monitors the systems, receives outage reports, prioritizes by severity, coordinates the technical team, and keeps customers updated. The result: faster response, fewer escalations, and a noticeably relieved support team.

About us

A product studio from Zug

utxo AG develops digital products and systems, from the first idea through development to ongoing operation. Our focus is applied AI: custom CoWorkers and products such as the Call Assistant and the Doc Indexer.

Behind this is enterprise experience: for the Serviceplan Group, Europe’s largest owner-run agency group, we develop and operate Masumi and Sokosumi. Sokosumi now has more than 13,000 registered users. Before that, we developed a fundraising platform for the UN refugee agency UNHCR, among other projects, and provided infrastructure for LCX, the regulated financial exchange in Liechtenstein.
