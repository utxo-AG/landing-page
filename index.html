A product studio from Zug

utxo AG guides companies through digital transformation, from idea through development to ongoing operation. Our focus is applied AI: custom CoWorkers and automations that move into your operations and work with your existing systems.

Behind it stands enterprise experience: with Masumi and Sokosumi we built the infrastructure of the AI agent economy, and we run it together with the Serviceplan Group, Europe's largest owner-run agency group. Before that, we built digital infrastructure for organizations ranging from the UN refugee agency UNHCR to the regulated financial exchange LCX in Liechtenstein.

A team that sticks with it

Not an anonymous outsourcing team. Developers, designers, and strategists who want to understand and ease your processes.

Projects

Masumi & Sokosumi

Serviceplan recognized the shift toward agentic AI early and built Masumi with us, an on-chain payment and identity network for AI agents, along with Sokosumi, a marketing platform where "agentic employees" handle campaign tasks end-to-end under human oversight. Both now run inside Serviceplan (House of Communication) in a GDPR- and EU-AI-Act-compliant setup — and have since been rolled out to their clients too.

NMKR-Studio

Our first project. We provide infrastructure for the chaotic world of NFTs. A SaaS platform that lets anyone easily create blockchain tokens and sell them conveniently via API or through our own payment portal.

We manage our customers' assets and process millions of requests, some of which reach us without warning. Reliability and resilience have defined our products since day one.

RST Datentechnik

For the regional internet provider RST Datentechnik on the Upper Rhine, we put a CoWorker into operation for outage management. It proactively monitors the systems, receives outage reports, prioritizes by severity, coordinates the technical team, and keeps customers updated. The result: faster response, fewer escalations, and a noticeably relieved support team.
